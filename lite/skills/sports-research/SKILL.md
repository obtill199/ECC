# Sports research

- Evidence first: role, injury, usage, schedule, market price if known.
- Separate fact, inference, and guess.
- No guaranteed-win or lock language. This harness is research, not a bankroll system.
- Keep notes short enough to use at a draft table.
- If the user has a dedicated picks/props system, defer sizing and official cards to that system instead of inventing one here.
