# HTML local service sites

Context: owner-operator sites. Goal is calls and form leads, not design awards.

Do:
- Keep phone number and primary CTA visible on mobile without hunting
- Write like a person in Wichita, not a national franchise mill
- Preserve existing visual language unless asked to redesign
- Check hrefs, tel: links, and form targets
- One conversion job per page (call, book, or inspect)

Do not:
- Introduce a JS framework
- Restyle the whole site for a copy change
- Invent certifications, reviews, or service areas
