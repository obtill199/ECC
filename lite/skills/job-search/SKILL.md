# Job search

- Never invent titles, dates, clearance, tools, or metrics.
- Score fit. Prefer >=70. State gaps.
- Reorder and rephrase real evidence only.
- Output a draft for human review, not an auto-apply packet.
- Do not scrape private boards or automate applications.
